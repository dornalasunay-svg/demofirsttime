The page skeleton

A browser-friendly document starts with these pieces

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
    <title>My page</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <header>...</header>
    <main>...</main>
    <footer>...</footer>
  </body>
</html>

02

Text & meaning

Give content the right structure, not just the right look

Paragraph and line break

A paragraph groups related sentences into a readable block.

Use a line break when a new line matters, like an address:
14 Market Street
Brooklyn, NY

<p>A paragraph of text.</p>
<p>14 Market Street<br>Brooklyn, NY</p>

Inline emphasis

Important information, emphasized words, highlighted text, and fine print.

Water is H2O; two squared is 22.

<strong>Important</strong>
<em>Emphasized</em>
<mark>Highlighted</mark>
<small>Fine print</small>
H<sub>2</sub>O · 2<sup>2</sup>

Quote and citation

Great things are done by a series of small things brought together.
Vincent van Gogh
<blockquote>
  Great things are done...
  <cite>Vincent van Gogh</cite>
</blockquote>

Code and separator

Use <code> for a short code fragment.

A thematic break uses <hr>.

<p>Run <code>npm test</code>.</p>
<hr>

Audio & video

Media controls appear when a source is provided.

<audio controls src="sound.mp3">
  Your browser does not support audio.
</audio>

<video controls width="320" poster="preview.jpg">
  <source src="clip.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>
